BrickStep Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

BrickStep is provided by Beijing Wuwei Rabbit Graphic Design Center (Sole Proprietorship). This policy explains how BrickStep handles information.

Summary

BrickStep works locally on the user's device. We do not operate an account system, cloud service, advertising network, or analytics service for BrickStep. We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data. The only optional network activity is Apple's StoreKit flow for the one-time Pro purchase and restore.

Information Handled on the Device

BrickStep may access model files, an optional external LDraw library, local favorites, recent-document bookmarks, and a viewport snapshot only when needed for features requested by the user. This information is processed locally and is not transmitted to us.

Sharing

Screenshots and exported CSV files leave BrickStep only when the user chooses a destination in the system share sheet or file exporter. We do not receive a copy.

In-App Purchases

BrickStep offers an optional one-time BrickStep Pro purchase. Purchases and restores are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive or store payment card details. The app may check Apple's signed transaction status to unlock Pro; no purchase data is sent to a developer-operated server.

Collection, Advertising, and Tracking

BrickStep does not collect data for App Store privacy-label purposes. It contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking technology.

Retention and Deletion

Local preferences remain on the device until removed in the app or the app is deleted. Files in document providers remain under the user's control.

Children

BrickStep does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because it does not collect personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy if data practices change. A revised policy will show a new effective date.
