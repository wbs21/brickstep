BrickStep Support

Help for BrickStep

Current requirements: iOS or iPadOS 18.0 or later on iPhone or iPad. The release is currently available in the United States, and the interface is in English.

View on the U.S. App Store Watch the demo

Supported Files

BrickStep opens local .io, .ldr, and .mpd files. Tap Open Model and choose a file from Files or another document provider.

Pricing and Imports

BrickStep is free to download and includes 10 model imports. A one-time $4.99 BrickStep Pro purchase removes the import limit. There is no subscription.

Only unique, successfully imported models use an import. Re-opening a model you already imported, cancelling an import, and files that fail to load do not use an import.

Models you already imported stay available without upgrading. Use Restore Purchases in Settings & Help or on the Pro screen to restore a previous purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open more than one model?

Yes. Use Open Model to choose another local file. Recent model bookmarks remain available on the device.

Do I need to upload my model?

No. BrickStep processes the selected file locally on the device. No account or developer-operated cloud service is required. A network connection is only needed for the optional LDraw.org links or an App Store purchase.

Why does my Studio file not look exactly like a Studio instruction page?

BrickStep is a 3D viewer, not a page-layout renderer. It uses Studio model.ins pages for step order and per-step camera views, and shows a parts callout for the current step. Custom page layouts, image callouts, and custom instruction graphics are not reproduced.

Where can I download models?

The LDraw Official Model Repository at omr.ldraw.org hosts free community recreations of LEGO sets in .mpd format, many with building steps. In BrickStep, open Settings & Help, tap Browse OMR Models, download a model, then tap Open a Downloaded Model. These are community models, not official LEGO instructions.

How do I export a parts list?

Open the BOM tab, choose the complete model or current step, search for a part or color if needed, and use the export control to save a CSV file.

What should I include in a compatibility report?

Include the file extension, approximate part and step counts, device model, OS version, and the error message. Do not share a private model file unless you are comfortable doing so.

Detailed Geometry

For complete part geometry, open Settings & Help, tap Download LDraw Library, unzip complete.zip in Files, then tap Choose LDraw Folder and select the ldraw folder containing parts and p. Access is limited to the folder selected by the user.

The download is about 140 MB and the unzipped library is about 530 MB. Studio .io files often include the parts they use; .ldr and .mpd files benefit most from the external library.

Gestures

Drag with one finger to orbit, pinch to zoom, and drag with two fingers to pan. Double-tap the viewport to reset the camera.

Large Models

BrickStep uses local resource safeguards for large files. If a model exceeds a limit, the app shows an error instead of continuing indefinitely. Very large historical geometry may use simplified display geometry.

Studio Content

BrickStep reads Studio model.ins pages for step order and camera views, and shows a parts callout for the current step. Custom page layouts, image callouts, and custom instruction graphics are not reproduced.

Settings & Help

The gear button opens setup guidance for the LDraw parts library and LDraw OMR, plus Pro status, step-view toggles, privacy, support, and rating links.
